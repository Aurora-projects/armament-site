2026.11.09ITEMS

ITEM REVAMP

Around a thousand new items: weapons, armour, food, drugs, gifts and tools.

Every weapon now has a family and a skill that makes it stronger as you use it: guns, bows, blades, bludgeons, saws, whips, psychic arms and charm. There are 172 new weapons. Guns are built from parts, and a gun now looks like the parts on it, firing from the end of whatever barrel it has. Bows, crossbows, slings, throwing knives, grenades, harpoons and boomerangs that come back to your hand join them. Melee covers blunt, slashing, piercing and serrated, fifteen of each, and every tech line hits in its own way. Whips shoot out, crack and snap back, and a chain whip can pull you up to a ledge.

Psychic weapons are the strange ones: orbs that follow your cursor and fire, staffs that cast, summons that fight beside you for a while, and tethers that drag an enemy into your grip. Charm weapons hit the mind instead of the body. A megaphone, a hypnotist's watch or a lute wears down someone's mental health and composure. Land one from stealth and they're talked down rather than killed, and none of it counts as assault. Composure is now a bar under your mind, and it's saved with you.

There are 40 new armour sets in eight kinds: armour, tactical, charismatic, spiritual, assassin, mechanical, biomancer and wizard. Each set gives a bonus at two pieces and at four. Armour is worn in four slots and drawn once, made to fit other bodies when they come.

Food fills a new hunger bar, and some of it comes with side effects. Medicine heals the body or the mind. Drugs help until you're hooked, then withdrawal hits whenever one wears off, until a doctor's detox or enough clean days clear it. Gifts are liked or hated by each personality, and there are gift shops around Venus now. Tools range from picks and drills, worn in their own slot to speed up mining, to shields, grappling hooks, smoke bombs, scanners, traps and a jetpack. Bags give you more room, and rings, amulets, belts and dyes round it out.

Anything you can build now has a blueprint to find or buy, and every recipe uses the ten core materials, apart from the rarest lines and some food. The workbenches and the debug item spawner were rebuilt to handle the numbers, with categories, search and a preview.
