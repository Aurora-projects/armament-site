The living ship lines

Four biological ship lines, each with a brain and a mind of its own.

Fleshmeld, Hidden, Wooded and Celicra join the five mechanical lines. Fleshmeld is cheap and raw: broken parts regrow as scar tissue between fights, and repairs can be paid in meat. Hidden looks like a plain mechanical hauler; heavy hits tear its panels open, and it reads as mechanical on scans until it's boarded. Wooded grows its own fuel, crops grow on its decks, it burns, and it gets tougher the longer it goes without a fight. Celicra is locked behind rare blueprints, heals itself, confuses whatever it hits, and can sing the Hymn. Every living ship has a mind: it grows through five tiers, works parts in a fight, follows orders as far as its bond allows, and you can talk to it.
