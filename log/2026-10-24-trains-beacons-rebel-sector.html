TRANSMISSIONS // dev log

2026.10.24VENUS

Trains, beacons and the rebel sector

A subway on a timetable, landing beacons, mechs and the rebels' home.

Two subway trains run round the Venus ring on one shared timetable; a ride costs a fare and moves the clock on by the real travel time. Landing beacons replaced picking a district from orbit: place one, name it, land beside it. The rebels got their sector: checkpoints, a depot, a farm, a training yard and a base in an old mall, a leader with missions, and three animals. Mechs arrived as the fourth aspect kind.
