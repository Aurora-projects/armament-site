SHIP OVERHAUL

A new card battle, life aboard, and work to pay for it all.

Ship combat is now a card game. Your deck comes from your ship's parts, so a gun adds gun cards, and they can't be played once it's broken. Fleets line up in lanes, and a won fight ends with a choice: salvage the wreck, or board it on foot and raid it. Boarding a ship that's still fighting means facing its whole crew. Shipyards now build mechanical ships and hatcheries build living ones, and only Lattice and Hidden parts mix with either.

Your ship helps you on the ground too. Hold the ability wheel to call in artillery, supply drops, medical drops, drones, shield domes and scans from orbit, and every ship line puts its own twist on one of them. Enemies with ships can call artillery on you as well.

The crew live aboard: they do maintenance, sleep in the bunks, eat at the galley and chat at the mess table, and they keep repairing the ship while you're away. Party members who aren't on your away team become crew, and you pick their jobs at the command console, which has its own look on every ship line. Living ships' minds now grow with you through the fights you win and the systems you see. A bonded mind warns you of ambushes, brings its own card into your deck, grows you gifts, and can hold the ship together when it would be disabled. Treat it badly and it sometimes refuses your calls.

And there's work. Dockmasters and faction leaders offer hauls: pack oddly shaped cargo into your hold like Tetris, and deliver it along a route. Hide contraband deep in the hold and get it past the law checks at planets, or pay the fine; what you can't pay becomes debt, and a quarter of what you earn goes to it until it's cleared. Lose a fight and pirates steal part of your cargo, then wait on a nearby planet for a limited time if you want it back. Bounty boards in government buildings post kills, captures, recoveries, espionage, negotiations won with charisma, deliveries, surveys and clear-outs, in space or on the ground. The galaxy map's new JOBS layer shows where all of it leads.
