2026.10.26SYSTEMS

Charisma

Talking becomes a combat system.

Every entity can now be persuaded, scared, recruited or shut down. Four rational types are played against seven logical positions: a fear play that works on a pack animal fails on something spiritual, and the metal hunger are algorithmic. The player gets a card hand (N) and a sentence minigame; enemies wind up cards of their own, and you can retort (F), walk out of earshot, or rely on your own stubbornness. Bosses refuse most actions until they're calm, then they'll parley.
