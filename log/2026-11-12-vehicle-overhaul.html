2026.11.12VEHICLES

VEHICLE OVERHAUL

Five lines of vehicles to build, cheap scrap cars at the dealer, and living mounts you steer with your nerves.

Vehicles used to be one kind of thing: the city's dragon-headed cars, kept in a garage. Now each line builds its own. The dragon look stays with the city's own dealer, Gilded Motors, which also sells five cheap scrap vehicles: a junker buggy, a hover crate, a drum trike, a patchwork van and a technical with a gun on the back.

A Mechanist builds vehicles at the Workshop, five for each tech line. Corpo makes clean pearl-white utility trucks, pods and an armoured executive car, plus a riot van that stops people without killing them. Military makes tracked carriers, a scout car, an APC that carries your party and a siege tank wide enough to fill two lanes. The Lattice makes rare, ornate machines of gold and light. Brassdan is an ocean people, so its six clockwork vehicles all work in water, and the sixth is a submarine.

A Biomancer grows mounts at the Vat instead: living creatures for Fleshmeld, Hidden and Celicra, five each, plus a silk spider that climbs walls and crosses ceilings. When you climb on, tendrils grow up around you and plug into your nerves. You steer it instantly, its organ gives it a power of its own, and you feel some of its pain until your bond grows. Feed it and it bonds with you. If it goes down it is carried home and regrows, so there's no repair bill like a car's. Each line cries in its own voice.

Every vehicle now works like the other aspects: build it, summon it, refit it at the bench, and copy it into a key fob to hand to a party member. A car you buy comes as its key fob. A parked car stays where you left it, even when you go indoors or travel far away, and every vehicle's body now matches its size.
