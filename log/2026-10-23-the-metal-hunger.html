2026.10.23VENUS

The metal hunger

A faction at war with everything, and the thing under the lab.

The abandoned district split in two: the rebel sector and the metal hunger sector, with a sky road running over both. The metal hunger live in their buildings, attack anything with a soul, and fight in packs that change tactics when a leader is near. Attacks, shots and spells no longer pass through walls. Deep under the abandoned laboratory sleeps the Ossuary Engine, a boss that has to be woken on purpose.
