DOSSIER // what it is

The cosmos is vast and delectable and only the most vicious shall have a seat at the banquet (what in the world are you talking about Sir?)

Armament is a sandbox RPG in the spirit of Terraria and Starbound, built in Godot 4.7 and grown out of a tabletop RPG system of the same name. It looks 2D, but the world is five lanes of cubes seen through a perspective camera, so you build, dig and fight in depth as well as side to side. Pick a race and a class, build a ship, and travel between the stars.

Almost everything is generated. A galaxy grows from a seed and a shape you pick or design yourself. Each system rolls its own stars, planets, moons and landing zones; each planet its look, weather, day and night, and biomes; each town its buildings, rooms and people, put together from reusable blueprints and data. Venus, the first place with a full story, is hand-built on the same generator, and the worlds after it are made the same way.

SYSTEMS ONLINE // features

  • BLOCK WORLD

    Five lanes of half-unit cubes under a perspective camera. Blocks have ramps, connected textures, random variants and glow. Liquids flow, powder falls, light is worked out on its own thread, and the world streams in chunks as you move. Buildings have rooms you walk into.

    lanes 5 · block 16 px · liquids flowing and still

  • PROCEDURAL UNIVERSE

    Galaxies come from a seed and a shape: spiral, grand, disc, or your own, built from layers of arms, bulge, bar, ring and halo with density formulas you write. Systems, planets, moons and landing zones roll from archetypes and templates, with layered planet looks, weather, day and night, and parallax backdrops.

    systems ~10,000 · shapes 3 + custom · seed: any number or word

  • TOWNS

    Settlements are generated to fit their biome from blueprints in five categories: workplace, leisure, vendor, governmental, residential. Every NPC gets a job, a bed, a daily schedule and one of 90 personality archetypes. Jobs, quests and shops are data on markers, so a new town reuses all of it.

    Venus districts 7 · archetypes 90 · building categories 5

  • SHIPS

    Ships are built cell by cell from part kits on a blueprint board, outside, as a blueprint, and inside. Ten lines of parts: five mechanical, four living, and Lattice. Living ships need a brain, and the brain has a mind with a bond, a mood and memories; it works parts in a fight and follows your orders. Fleets, ship combat and boarding are in.

    lines 10 · parts per line 17 · living: Fleshmeld, Hidden, Wooded, Celicra

  • CLASSES & GRAFTS

    Six classes: Brawler, Psychic, Slinger, Nano Mage, Mechanist and Biomancer, with levels, 20 feats and 30 proficiencies. 97 cybernetic and genetic modifications go onto real body parts, limited by each race's cyber and gene load. Guns are built from parts at a gun bench.

    classes 6 · mods 97 · feats 20 · proficiencies 30

  • NANO MAGIC

    The Nano Mage programs spells. A spell is a chain of runes with yes / no branches, splits and loops, edited as a numbered stack or as a flow chart, and tried on two dummies in a test range, with an energy timeline, before you cast it for real.

    views: stack, grid · test range: play, step, reset

  • MECHS & VEHICLES

    A mech is seven slots: chassis, engine, an arm on each mouse button, legs that walk, slither, float or climb walls, and two accessories. Mechanists and Biomancers build them; the rebels sell them. Cars are modular too: every part is its own picture on the shell, so a refit changes the look and the numbers.

    mech slots 7 · mech parts 21 · car models 5

  • CHARISMA

    Talking is a combat system. Four rational types (Logos, Pathos, Cathos, Fearacon) work or fail against seven logical positions. Play actions from a card hand and land them in a sentence minigame while you keep moving. Enemies do it to you, and you can retort. Bosses can be calmed into a parley.

    actions 41 · types 4 · positions 7

  • LAW & FACTIONS

    Crimes get seen and reported. Assault gets you detained and jailed, murder gets you killed, and you can pay bail, confess or stay wanted. Factions keep reputation, hold turf and charge strangers a toll. The metal hunger are at war with everyone.

    jail, bail, fines · protectors: sheriffs, enforcers, turrets
